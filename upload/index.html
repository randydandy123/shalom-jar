Shalom upload sheet

2 reels + 2 carousels

For Gali. Four posts for @shalom_elbert, each scheduled in the Instagram app. Do them in any order, the time on each card is what matters.

  1. 1.Open the card's links and save the files to your phone (video or slides, plus the cover).
  2. 2.In Instagram, logged in as @shalom_elbert, tap + and pick the files. Carousel slides go in number order, 01 first.
  3. 3.Tap Copy caption on the card and paste it. Tap Add location and choose New York, New York.
  4. 4.Reels only: tap Edit cover, then Add from camera roll, and pick the cover you saved.
  5. 5.Tap More options (Advanced settings), turn on Schedule this post, set the date and time from the card, then Schedule.
  6. 6.Tick the box at the bottom of the card.
1.How to make a brine reelWed 11 Nov

4.The Fermentulator carouselWed 18 Nov

All four go out at 19:30 Israel time, which is 12:30 New York time. Instagram uses your phone's clock, so pick the one your phone is set to.
